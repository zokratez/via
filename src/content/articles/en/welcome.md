Every day, peptide studies get published in journals your pharmacy will never read. Retatrutide, cagrilintide, the next twenty compounds — the information exists, but it's buried in PDFs behind academic paywalls, in conferences no member of the public is invited to, in preprints even doctors don't have time to review.

PACO Peptide is the library that was missing. Bukowski reads what you can't — the trials, the preliminary publications, the scientific conversations happening in real time — and translates them without the jargon, without the agenda, without five pages of disclaimers.

We don't sell peptides. We don't prescribe. We don't have a supplier to recommend. The only thing we have is the information your doctor doesn't have time to review — and the honesty to tell you when we don't know something yet.

This is the first entry. More are coming.
